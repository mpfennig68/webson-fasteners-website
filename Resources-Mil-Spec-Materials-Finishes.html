// READING THE CALLOUT

Read the requirement in three layers

Material and finish layers
ItemReference
Base materialThe alloy or material grade of the part.
Condition and performanceHeat treatment, hardness, strength and other requirements stated by the controlling specification.
Surface treatment and lubricantPassivation, plating, supplemental treatment and lubrication, where required.

These layers work together. A matching thread size and a similar-looking finish do not establish the same fastener requirement. Send Webson the complete designation and any drawing notes so the full combination can be reviewed.

// BASE MATERIAL

Common material descriptions

Mil-Spec material terminology
TermWhat it tells youWhat to confirm
CRES / stainless steelCRES means corrosion-resistant steel. It describes a material family, rather than a single alloy or strength level.Retain the exact alloy, condition and product specification. A general stainless description does not establish an equivalent part.
303 / 303SeThese are specific corrosion-resistant steel designations used in some hardware families.Preserve the grade called out by the selected series and any material suffix.
Alloy steelA base-material description used in specification-controlled hardware. The grade and heat treatment remain part of the requirement.Provide the required grade, strength or hardness condition and protective treatment.
A286A distinct alloy designation used in aerospace hardware, including some threaded-insert options.Keep the specified material condition and processing requirements; do not substitute a generic stainless description.
Other specified alloysA drawing may identify aluminum, brass, nickel-copper or another material.Quote the complete alloy and condition as specified, rather than selecting material by appearance.

// FINISH REQUIREMENTS

Common surface treatments and lubricants

Mil-Spec finish and lubricant terminology
TreatmentWhat it describesWhat to send Webson
PassivationA treatment for specified corrosion-resistant steels that addresses surface contamination such as free iron. It is distinct from depositing a metal plating.Supply the required process specification, method/type and verification requirements when stated.
Cadmium platingAn electrodeposited metal coating that appears in some military hardware requirements.Retain the exact specification, type, class and any additional processing requirements. Ask Webson to review any proposed alternative.
Zinc-nickel platingA separate coating designation from cadmium or ordinary zinc plating. Some series define specific options.Do not replace one coating with another using color or appearance. Provide the exact callout and application restrictions.
Dry-film lubricantA surface-applied lubricant that may be specified in addition to a base material or coating.Identify the lubricant specification and type. Locking and lubrication requirements may depend on the application.
Other coating or treatmentThe finish may be controlled by a drawing note, part-number suffix or referenced process specification.Include the complete requirement, including any supplemental treatment. A finish name alone may be incomplete.

// SERIES-SPECIFIC EXAMPLE

One family shows why the suffix matters

MS51830 keylocking inserts distinguish material, coating and internal locking options within a single series. Revision G includes CA for A286 and QX for zinc-nickel plated alloy steel. Its L and LM locking suffixes distinguish aerospace and non-aerospace lubrication requirements.

Those codes belong to that series. They should not be copied into other MS, NAS or AN part numbers as universal material or finish rules.

// DISCUSS THE REQUIREMENT

Questions to resolve before changing a finish

  • Does the drawing permit an alternative material or coating?
  • Does the proposed part preserve dimensions, thread fit and specified performance?
  • Are application-specific lubrication or locking requirements retained?
  • Will the required certificates and processing records be available?
  • Who must approve the change before purchase?

If a finish is difficult to source, ask Webson to review the exact requirement. Any proposed alternative should be identified separately for the appropriate approval.
