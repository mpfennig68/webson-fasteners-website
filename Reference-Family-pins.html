// IDENTIFICATION

Choose by the retention and fit requirement.

Dowel, spring, cotter, clevis and quick-release pins serve different locating or retention tasks. Start with the pin type called out on the drawing.

MS16555 and MS16556 distinguish different over-nominal dowel families. A slotted tubular spring pin such as MS16562 is a different product, not an oversized solid dowel.

// PREPARE AN RFQ

What to confirm on the drawing

Pin style; diameter and tolerance; grip/length; end features; holes; material and protective treatment.

  • Complete designation, including all material, finish and locking suffixes.
  • Quantity, required delivery date and controlling drawing or specification issue.
  • Required certificates, source restrictions and lot traceability.
