// MORE THAN A PART NUMBER

A qualification worth asking about.

When you compare fastener suppliers, look beyond the part number and delivery date. Ask how the distributor manages the material, its source, and the records behind your shipment.

AS9120B is a quality management system standard written for aviation, space, and defense distributors. It incorporates ISO 9001 requirements and adds requirements for this supply chain.1 For Webson Fasteners, certification provides an independently assessed foundation for the distribution work your order depends on.

// WHAT IT MEANS FOR YOUR ORDER

Three things that matter to a buyer.

01

Traceability

AS9120B includes enhanced traceability and documentation requirements.2 Ask how the offered material connects to its manufacturer and supporting records, including lot identification where applicable.

Your next step: Specify the manufacturer certificates, lot records, or reports you need before ordering.

02

Counterfeit prevention

The standard addresses prevention of counterfeit and suspect unapproved parts. IAQG guidance connects this work to source selection, supplier controls, and handling of suspect material.3

Your next step: Ask how a supplier approves sources and responds when markings or paperwork raise a concern.

03

Documented controls

Certification puts the quality management system under third-party assessment.4 Buyers should ask how their order requirements are reviewed, recorded, and carried through to shipment.

Your next step: Put drawing revisions, source restrictions, and approval requirements into the RFQ.

// KEEP THIS CHECKLIST

Four questions for any distributor.

Use these questions when adding a supplier or reviewing your current sources. Ask for clear answers before issuing the purchase order.

  1. Can I review your current certificate and its scope?
  2. Who manufactured the offered material?
  3. Which documents will accompany this shipment?
  4. Will you identify exceptions or proposed substitutions before I order?
