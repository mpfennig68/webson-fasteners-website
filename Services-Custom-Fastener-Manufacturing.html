How the order moves from drawing to delivery

  1. Requirement and source review

    Webson coordinates review of the drawing and purchasing requirements with manufacturing sources. Any open requirements need to be resolved for an agreed quote.

  2. Quotation

    Review the offered quantity, pricing, lead time, tooling or setup charges, and stated documentation. Identify any proposed deviation before ordering.

  3. Production and secondary operations

    Webson coordinates manufacturing and the agreed heat treatment, plating, coating, thread treatment or other specified processing through the supply chain.

  4. Documentation and delivery

    The order requirements guide the supplied records, identification and packaging. For repeat purchases, reference the applicable part number and drawing revision again.

Plan repeat production when you request the first quote

Include estimated annual usage, release sizes and packaging requirements if the part will be ordered repeatedly. Those details help evaluate the manufacturing route and purchasing program alongside the initial order quantity.

Questions before you request a quote

Does Webson manufacture custom fasteners in-house?

Webson is a distributor and coordinates custom production through qualified manufacturing partners. Manufacturing and processing options are reviewed against the individual drawing and order requirements.

Which manufacturing processes can be reviewed?

Depending on geometry, material and quantity, Webson can coordinate turning, milling, cold forming, thread rolling or cutting, broaching and modification of standard parts. The production route is reviewed for the specific component.

Can you coordinate finishing and heat treatment?

Yes. Identify the required plating, coating, heat treatment, post-plating bake, thread patch or other secondary operation and its governing specification. Availability and pricing depend on the part and source.

Can you provide certifications and traceability?

Identify required certificates, material records, process documentation, inspection reports and traceability requirements with the RFQ. The agreed requirements should also appear on the purchase order.

How are minimum quantity and lead time determined?

They depend on the drawing, material, process, tooling, finishing and source availability. Submit your required quantity and delivery date for review; the quote will establish the offered terms.

Can repeat orders be scheduled?

Webson can review blanket orders, scheduled releases, kitting and inventory programs for recurring requirements. Include forecast usage, release quantities and packaging needs so the program can be evaluated.

Send the drawing and the requirements together

Start with the part number, drawing revision, quantity and requested delivery date. Include material, finish, critical tolerances and required certifications. For repeat orders, add annual usage and release quantities if known.
