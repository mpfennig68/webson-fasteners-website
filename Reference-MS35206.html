// PART IDENTIFICATION

Pan head, cross recess and coarse thread

MS35206 identifies cadmium-plated steel, cross-recessed pan-head machine screws with UNC-2A threads. The raised pan head sits above its bearing surface.

Head style, drive, thread, material and finish all belong in the requirement.

// LENGTH REFERENCE

Measure length beneath the head

For these pan-head screws, nominal length runs from the bearing surface beneath the head to the tip. MS35206-213 is a 4-40 by 1/4-inch selection; MS35206-228 is a 6-32 by 3/8-inch selection.
