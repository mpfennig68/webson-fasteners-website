Check the finished cutout and both sides of the panel

Measure the opening after any finish that affects the fit. Include the thickness, available rear space and allowable projection at the face. A larger head does not mean the plug fits a larger hole.

Identify what the closure needs to do

State whether the plug is for appearance, edge closure or a specified sealing or protection function. Include material, temperature and exposure requirements. If a particular enclosure rating is involved, preserve the approved plug and installation configuration.

Unused holes in equipment panels

Hole plugs can close unused mounting or cable-entry openings within a defined panel design. For replacement parts, send the complete manufacturer number if available. A photo can show the style, but dimensions and requirements are needed to identify the fit.

Before you order

Can I use the head diameter to select a plug?

No. Start with the mounting hole and panel thickness, then check the head and projection.

Does a plug make the opening watertight?

Only rely on the rating and installation conditions of the exact product and enclosure arrangement.

Can a round plug close a shaped cutout?

Use the plug specified for the actual cutout. A similar overall width does not establish retention or fit.

Include hole shape and panel thickness

Send the part number or drawing, quantity, delivery date, cutout, panel thickness, material and allowable projection. Add any sealing, rating or documentation requirements.
