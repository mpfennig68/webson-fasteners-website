Check stack, hole and blind-side space

Two rivets with the same body diameter can have different grip ranges, heads, materials or setting requirements. Do not use the apparent length of an installed rivet to identify the original part. Start with the drawing or manufacturer designation.

Body and mandrel materials are separate details

A rivet description may identify more than one material because the body and mandrel can differ. Preserve that combination, along with finish and required documentation. For dissimilar materials or exposure conditions, follow the approved joint requirement.

Permanent fastening and threaded attachment are different choices

Blind rivets make a set joint. Rivet nuts provide a threaded point for a separate screw. If the panel must be removed regularly, identify that service requirement rather than treating the two products as substitutes.

Before you order

Is grip range the same as rivet length?

No. Grip range states the material thickness the selected rivet is designed to fasten. Rivet length is a separate dimension.

Does the nominal rivet diameter define the drill size?

Use the specified hole size and tolerance for the exact product. A nominal diameter alone is not the complete hole requirement.

Can a closed-end rivet guarantee a sealed enclosure?

The complete joint and installed product determine sealing. Include the required rating or acceptance test with the part requirement.
