// READING THE COMPARISON

The same screw thread can require a different outside fit

For a 1/4-20 internal thread, MS51830-106 has a 3/8-16 external thread, MS51831-102 has 7/16-14, and MS51832-102 has 1/2-13. All three selected references have a nominal 0.370-inch length. Their outside fits are different.

The table covers nonlocking internal threads. Do not add L or carry another material or lubrication suffix across a manufacturer number without reviewing that configuration. Published lengths are identification references, not hole-preparation or installation instructions.
