// PART IDENTIFICATION

Identify the nut and its configuration

MS51972 identifies plain hexagon nuts in passivated 300-series corrosion-resisting steel with UNF-2B internal threads. The table covers all 14 reviewed Table I selections from 1/4-28 through 1-1/2-12. These are plain nuts; the family does not provide a prevailing-torque locking feature.

// ORDERING EXAMPLES

Read the complete designation

MS51972-1 identifies a 1/4-28 nut; MS51972-5 is 1/2-20. At 7/16-20, MS51972-4 has a 0.6750–0.6875-inch width across flats. Do not copy the smaller hex-head dimensions of a similarly sized cap screw into the nut requirement.

// MATERIAL AND FINISH

Preserve the material and finish

The reviewed issue specifies 300-series corrosion-resisting steel and passivation. The family number alone does not identify a particular 300-series grade. Include any specific material, finish and certification requirement with the inquiry.

// FAMILY COMPARISON

Compare thread, envelope and part status

MS51968 is a cadmium-plated carbon-steel fine-thread family with its own grade requirements. MS35650 is a fine-thread machine-screw nut family. Matching a nominal thread does not establish equal material, strength, thickness or wrench clearance.
