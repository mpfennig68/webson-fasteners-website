// ELECTRONIC HARDWARE

Mounting and fastening components from one source.

Electronic assemblies rely on a wide variety of small components to position circuit boards, secure panels, protect pass-through points and provide access to equipment. A missing spacer, standoff or panel component can delay an otherwise complete build.

Webson supplies and sources electronic hardware for board-level assemblies, chassis, panels, racks, instrumentation and equipment enclosures. Customers can submit individual part numbers, drawings or a complete bill of material for review.

Standoffs

Threaded mounting components used to establish controlled spacing between circuit boards, panels, chassis and other assembly surfaces.

Spacers

Threaded and unthreaded components used to maintain separation, alignment and support within electronic and mechanical assemblies.

Handles and Knobs

Operating and access hardware for equipment fronts, control panels, drawers, racks, instruments and enclosures.

Bushings and Insulators

Components used to protect wires, isolate conductive surfaces and support pass-through points in panels and chassis.

Latches and Captive Panel Hardware

Access and retention hardware for removable covers, service panels, enclosures and equipment assemblies.

Precision Screws and Fasteners

Machine screws, captive fasteners and related mounting hardware for PCB, chassis, panel and electronic equipment assembly.

// FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Electronic hardware questions.

What is the difference between a spacer and a standoff?

A spacer maintains a defined distance between components and may be threaded or unthreaded. A standoff is generally threaded and is used to fasten and position components such as a circuit board and chassis. The correct configuration depends on the assembly design and mounting requirements.

Does Webson supply both metal and insulating electronic hardware?

Yes. Webson supplies machined and molded electronic hardware in conductive and insulating configurations. Material availability depends on the component, dimensions, part number and application requirements.

Can Webson source custom-length spacers and standoffs?

Webson can review requirements for custom lengths, thread sizes, materials and configurations. Send the applicable drawing, dimensions, quantity and material requirements for review.

Can Webson source manufacturer part numbers that are not shown on the website?

Yes. The website represents only a portion of Webson's sourcing capabilities. Submit the complete manufacturer part number, required quantity and any applicable documentation requirements for review.

Can electronic hardware be included in a vendor-managed inventory program?

Yes. Recurring electronic hardware and fastener requirements can be evaluated for vendor-managed inventory, bin-stocking, scheduled-release and custom-kitting programs.

What information should I include with an electronic hardware RFQ?

Provide the manufacturer part number or drawing, quantity, dimensions, thread configuration, material, finish and required delivery date whenever available. Drawings and supporting specifications can be attached to Webson's RFQ form.

Does Webson provide traceability documentation for electronic hardware?

Traceability and order documentation can be supplied according to the component and customer requirements. Include all documentation requirements with the RFQ and purchase order.
