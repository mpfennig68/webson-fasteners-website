Define all three primary washer dimensions

Inside diameter, outside diameter and thickness identify the basic envelope. A nominal screw-size label does not state every one of those values. For a shim, the tolerance on thickness may be as important as the nominal thickness.

Do not infer performance from the name

“Lock washer” is not a complete specification. Identify the actual product or standard and the approved installation arrangement. For bearing washers, include any hardness requirement. For insulating components, specify the resin or compound and required documentation.

Keep washer details on the purchase list

Washer substitutions can change the stack height, bearing area or contact material even when the screw still passes through the hole. List the specified washer with its mating fasteners, and identify whether alternatives may be proposed for approval.

Before you order

Can I order by screw size alone?

Use the complete washer designation where possible. Bore, outside diameter, thickness and material can vary for the same nominal screw size.

Is a shim washer the same as a general flat washer?

A shim is selected for controlled spacing and may have tighter dimensional requirements. Compare the actual drawing and tolerances.

Can any washer replace a specified locking system?

No. Preserve the system and installation requirements unless the design authority approves a change.

Include dimensions and material controls

Send the standard designation or drawing revision, quantity, delivery date, bore, outside diameter and thickness. Add material, hardness, finish and required inspection documents.
