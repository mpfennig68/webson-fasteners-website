// IDENTIFICATION

Preserve head style, material and installation requirements.

Solid rivets and blind rivets use different installation methods. A flush head also requires a compatible seat; head angle and head dimensions are part of the selected family.

MS20426 is a countersunk precision-head family and MS20470 is a universal-head family. Do not select between them by diameter and length alone.

// PREPARE AN RFQ

What to confirm on the drawing

Solid/blind type; head geometry; material code; diameter; length or grip; finish; installation process.

  • Complete designation, including all material, finish and locking suffixes.
  • Quantity, required delivery date and controlling drawing or specification issue.
  • Required certificates, source restrictions and lot traceability.
