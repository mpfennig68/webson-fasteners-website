// PART IDENTIFICATION

Retain the material and locking identifiers

NAS1352 covers coarse-thread socket-head screws. In the selected entries, C identifies corrosion-resistant steel and N identifies heat-resistant steel. Their listed thread reference is UNRC-3A.

// PART-NUMBER EXAMPLES

Read size and length separately

NAS1352C04-9 identifies 4-40 by 9/16 inch. NAS1352N6-16 identifies 3/8-16 by 1 inch. Preserve leading zeroes in the size identifier.

NAS1352C02LB4 retains LB, the locking-patch identifier, and its 1/4-inch nominal length.
