// MEASUREMENT GUIDE

Measure length and point geometry

L is overall screw length from the socket end to the flat point. C is the flat point diameter; J is nominal socket width across flats. The point and socket dimensions describe different ends of the screw.

The flat contact end differs from a recessed cup point and a projecting half-dog point. Confirm the required contact geometry before comparing replacement sizes.

// ORDERING DETAILS

Read the dash number and locking requirement

The selected MS51031B rows are self-locking references in the 10-32 and 1/4-28 columns. Locking is encoded by the table’s dash number, not by adding an L suffix. For example, MS51031-107 is a self-locking 10-32 screw with a 0.500-inch length. The reviewed drawing’s asterisked inactive-for-new-design selections are outside this table.

The locking element, effective locking area and torque provisions remain subject to the governing procurement issue and assembly requirements. This selected table does not supply a tightening torque.
