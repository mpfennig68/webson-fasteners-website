Choose the insert construction

Search selected inch-series screw-locking inserts by MS or NAS designation, KATO CoilThread number, Heli-Coil number or Recoil number. The tables cover 1D and 2D installed-length selections in bulk supply format, without the optional cadmium or dry-film finish suffixes.

Each table stays within one construction. A tanged MS21209 insert and a tangless NAS1130 insert are not presented as automatic substitutes. Installed lengths below are nominal diameter multiples; they are not free-coil lengths or tapped-hole depths.
