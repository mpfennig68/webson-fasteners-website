// PART IDENTIFICATION

Keep the locking construction in the callout

NAS1291 is a low-height, lightweight self-locking hex-nut family. The C3M and C4M parts shown here use all-metal construction in A286 with an approved dry-film lubricant. They are different selections from the MS21083 nonmetallic-insert family.

Check the thread, hex clearance, nut height, material and surface treatment against the drawing. Thread size alone does not establish an acceptable replacement.

// PART-NUMBER EXAMPLES

C3M and C4M identify different thread sizes

NAS1291C3M identifies the 10-32 selection; NAS1291C4M identifies 1/4-28. For these references, C identifies A286 and the M ending is associated with the dry-film-lubricated configuration.

Retain the complete suffix. A NAS1291C4 callout without M is a different configuration in the reviewed standard; it should not be quoted as interchangeable with NAS1291C4M.
