// IDENTIFICATION

Keep strength, thread and drilling requirements together.

The hex head is only one part of the requirement. Stainless, nickel-copper and carbon/alloy-steel cap screws are different families, with additional finish and drilled-feature requirements.

MS35307 and MS35308 are related corrosion-resistant families distinguished by thread series. MS90725 and MS90726 are cataloged Grade 5 families; they are not generic substitutes for stainless cap screws.

// PREPARE AN RFQ

What to confirm on the drawing

Grade/material; diameter and pitch; thread class; length; head/shank drilling; coating and test requirements.

  • Complete designation, including all material, finish and locking suffixes.
  • Quantity, required delivery date and controlling drawing or specification issue.
  • Required certificates, source restrictions and lot traceability.
