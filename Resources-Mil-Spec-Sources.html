// SOURCE REGISTER

Catalog and drawing references

Catalog pages identify hardware families and nominal entries. DLA records identify official document status. Where a historical drawing is used, the technical page identifies its revision explicitly.

Kanebridge Military Hardware catalog (2015)

Historical family descriptions; not current stock or compliance evidence.

Global Supply military document library ↗

Family index and links to public military drawings; archive copies may be historical.

Lyn-Tron NAS and shoulder-screw catalog ↗

Manufacturer catalog descriptions and nominal shoulder dimensions.

American Ring Mil-Spec catalog ↗

Manufacturer index of retaining ring families.

Acme Industrial - public MS51830F drawing ↗

US military drawing MS51830F (2012), Tables I-II and part-number note; historical revision explicitly identified.

MF Supply MS51576 dimensional chart ↗

Nominal shoulder dimensions, cross-checked against manufacturer catalog entries.

Lyn-Tron MS51576 manufacturer catalog ↗

Catalog nominal dimensions, not full tolerances or acceptance data.

Lyn-Tron MS51575 manufacturer catalog ↗

Selected listed catalog entries; dash 26 is intentionally not included in the extracted sample.

DLA ASSIST NASM16562 ↗

Active adoption record for slotted tubular spring pins.

MS20426L cancellation notice, August 18, 1998 ↗

Notice states the standard moved to NASM20426 while part numbers remained unchanged.

DLA MIL-HDBK-57 ↗

Manufacturer identification symbols; guidance, not an acceptance specification.
