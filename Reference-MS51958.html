// PART IDENTIFICATION

Identify the thread, head and material

MS51958 identifies cross-recessed pan-head machine screws in corrosion-resistant steel with UNF-2A threads. This selection covers dash numbers 1–119, from 2-64 through 3/8-24. MS51957 is the related coarse-thread family; its dash numbers do not identify the same sizes.

// LENGTH CONVENTION

Measure the correct length

Length is measured beneath the head to the tip. MS51958-13 is a 4-48 by 1/4-inch reference; MS51958-63 is a 10-32 by 1/2-inch reference. The reviewed drawing also contains 0-80 entries, outside this selected table. Historical manufacturer production annotations are not a statement of Webson inventory.

// ORDERING DETAILS

Keep finish and locking codes with the part

The reviewed MS51958C reference specifies 300-series corrosion-resistant steel and a cleaned and descaled surface treatment. A B following the dash number identifies black oxide; for example, MS51958-45B. The table lists unsuffixed reference sizes. Retain the finish and governing issue called out by your drawing.
