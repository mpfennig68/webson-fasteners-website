// PART IDENTIFICATION

Identify the thread, head and material

MS35207 identifies cross-recessed pan-head machine screws in carbon steel, cadmium plated, with UNF-2A threads. The table covers dash numbers 201–319, from 2-64 through 3/8-24. These are separate thread and dash-number selections from the coarse-thread MS35206 family.

// LENGTH CONVENTION

Measure the correct length

Length is measured from the bearing surface beneath the head to the tip. MS35207-202 is a 2-64 by 3/16-inch reference; MS35207-259 is a 10-32 by 1/4-inch reference. Fractional lengths below are nominal size names corresponding to the drawing’s decimal length rows.

Keep finish and locking codes with the part

The reviewed MS35207F notes specify cadmium plating to QQ-P-416, Type II, Class 3. Its part-number example is MS35207-201. Include any additional finish or drawing requirements with your inquiry.
