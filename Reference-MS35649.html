// PART IDENTIFICATION

Identify the nut family

MS35649 covers plain hexagon machine-screw nuts with UNC-2B internal threads. This selection includes 37 material and finish configurations from 2-56 through 3/8-16. These are plain nuts; they do not provide the prevailing-torque locking feature found in the MS21042 or MS21044 families.

// ORDERING EXAMPLES

Read the complete part number

MS35649-222 is a 2-56 carbon-steel nut with cadmium plating; MS35649-224 is the passivated CRES version. MS35649-225B identifies brass with black oxide, while MS35649-225T identifies tin-plated brass. The finish letter is part of the designation.

// MATERIAL AND FINISH

Retain the material and finish requirement

The selected carbon-steel dash numbers distinguish cadmium plating from phosphate coating; neither uses an added finish letter. The CRES rows are passivated. Brass rows use B for black oxide or T for tin plating. Other configurations in the standard are outside this selection. Preserve all finish letters instead of reducing the order to a bare dash number.

// FAMILY COMPARISON

Compare related nut families

MS35650 is the fine-thread machine-screw nut family. MS51967 is a finished hex nut family with separate strength grades and thickness requirements. For example, MS35649-2252 and MS51967-2 both have 1/4-20 threads, but their thickness limits differ.
