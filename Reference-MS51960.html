Identify the thread, head and material

// LENGTH CONVENTION

Measure the correct length

Overall length includes the countersunk head. The length column preserves the decimal values printed in MS51960D Table I, including 0.12, 0.19 and 0.62; these are reference table values, not an added acceptance tolerance. MS51960-65 identifies a 10-32 by 0.50-inch reference.

// ORDERING DETAILS

Keep finish and locking codes with the part

MS51960D specifies 300-series corrosion-resistant steel and passivation. B identifies black oxide and L identifies a self-locking element; the order is BL when both apply. MS51960-65BL is the drawing’s example of a 10-32, 0.50-inch screw with black oxide and a self-locking element. Suffixes are not interchangeable with the unsuffixed references in this table.
