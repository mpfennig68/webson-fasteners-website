Check the bundle, strap and mounting point

Include sleeving and protective layers in the finished bundle size. If a tie mounts to a panel or base, identify that attachment separately. A cable tie’s stated strength is not automatically a rating for the complete installed wiring support.

Identify the grade needed for the environment

Include the material designation and required temperature, outdoor exposure, flammability or other criteria. A black tie is not automatically a particular UV-resistant grade. For adhesive mounting bases, identify the base and adhesive system as well as the tie.

Production wiring and service access

Cable ties bundle and organize wiring in chassis, cabinets and electronic equipment. Releasable designs may suit areas opened during service; other installations call for a fixed arrangement. List the tie and mounting accessory together so the complete requirement can be quoted.

Before you order

Can I select a tie by overall length alone?

Use the stated bundle range, strap dimensions and material as well. Overall length does not directly state installed bundle capacity.

Does black mean UV resistant?

Use the manufacturer’s material and product designation. Color alone does not establish the required outdoor performance.

Are mounting bases universal?

Check the permitted tie dimensions, attachment method and installation conditions for the selected base.

Include ties and mounts in the same inquiry

Send full part numbers, quantity and delivery date. If no part number is available, include bundle range, strap dimensions, locking style, material and mounting details, plus any documentation requirements.
