Geometry and working position belong together

Outside diameter, inside diameter, thickness and free height describe important geometry. They do not replace a load-deflection requirement or identify every permitted operating condition. Use the manufacturer data or controlled spring drawing.

Keep material and operating conditions with the spring

Include material, heat treatment or condition, finish and any lubrication requirement. Add temperature, cycling and environmental criteria where controlled. A similar conical washer is not automatically equivalent to a specified disc spring.

Springs within a controlled assembly

Disc springs are used where the design calls for a defined spring response within the available space. Send the approved stack or assembly drawing when the order includes more than one disc. Any proposed change needs the complete spring requirement, not just a dimensional comparison.
