// WHY THE COMPLETE PART MATTERS

A shared dash number can hide a different length

MS51576-16 and MS51575-16 both have a 1/4-inch shoulder diameter, but their nominal shoulder lengths are 3/16 and 1/8 inch respectively. At a 1/4-inch shoulder diameter and 3/16-inch shoulder length, the selected pair is MS51576-16 and MS51575-17.
