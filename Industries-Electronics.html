Fastening hardware for electronics manufacturing.

Webson supplies the hardware that holds PCB assemblies, chassis, and interconnect systems together, sourced from a single certified distributor.

← All industries

Electronics manufacturers work in high-mix, high-volume production, where a missing spacer or an out-of-spec screw can stop an assembly line. Webson supplies the fastening and mounting hardware for board-level assembly, panel and chassis builds, and enclosure work, backed by the same AS9120B and ISO 9001 quality system we use for our aerospace and mil-spec lines.

Applications we support

  • PCB and board-level assembly
  • Chassis and panel manufacturing
  • Cable and interconnect systems
  • Test, rack, and instrumentation equipment
  • Consumer and industrial electronics housings

Fasteners & hardware for electronics manufacturing

Our electronics hardware line covers the parts that go into board-level assembly and panel work alike:

  • Spacers & standoffs: board-to-chassis mounting, machined and molded
  • Handles & knobs: equipment fronts, panels, and enclosures
  • Bushings: insulating and feedthrough hardware
  • Latches: access panels and removable covers
  • Precision machine screws & captive fasteners: for tight-tolerance assembly

See the full range on our products page.

Why electronics manufacturers choose Webson

Since 1986, Webson has supplied fastening hardware to electronic product manufacturers alongside our aerospace and medical customers. The same AS9120B / ISO 9001 traceability and documentation on a mil-spec order is available on a run of standoffs. Our value-added services, including vendor-managed inventory, bin stocking, and custom kitting, are built for the high-mix production schedules common in electronics manufacturing.
