// IDENTIFICATION

Keep bolt length, grip and drilling details separate.

The unthreaded grip is distinct from the full under-head length. Compare both measurements with the drawing.

AN3 and AN4 use different diameters and threads. A and H identifiers distinguish drilling configurations; use the complete designation.

// PREPARE AN RFQ

What to confirm on the drawing

Thread; under-head length; unthreaded grip; drilled head/shank; material; finish; documentation.

  • Complete designation, including all material, finish and locking suffixes.
  • Quantity, required delivery date and controlling drawing or specification issue.
  • Required certificates, source restrictions and lot traceability.
