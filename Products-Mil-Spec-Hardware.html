// MILITARY SPECIFICATION HARDWARE

Source Mil-Spec hardware from one certified distributor.

Aerospace and defense bills of material can contain hardware governed by several different specification systems. Webson helps customers consolidate those requirements through one experienced source.

Provide the complete part number, quantity, revision requirements and required documentation. Our team will review the requirement and source the hardware to the applicable specification.

AN

Army-Navy specification hardware commonly used in aircraft, airframe and aerospace assemblies.

MS

Military Standard hardware developed for repeatable use across military equipment and systems.

NAS

National Aerospace Standards covering fasteners and components used throughout the aerospace industry.

NASM

Military specifications maintained within the National Aerospace Standards system.

The controlling specification, drawing and purchase-order requirements determine the correct part. Customers should provide complete part numbers and revision information whenever available.

// FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Mil-Spec fastener questions.

What is the difference between AN, MS, NAS and NASM hardware?

AN, MS, NAS and NASM identify different families of military and aerospace specifications. Each family contains individual standards that define dimensions, materials, finishes, performance requirements and other characteristics. The complete part number and current specification determine the correct component.

Can Webson supply documentation with Mil-Spec fasteners?

Yes. Webson supplies aerospace-grade traceability and applicable order documentation based on the part specification and customer requirements. Include all certification and documentation requirements with the RFQ and purchase order.

Can Webson source Mil-Spec part numbers that are not listed on the website?

Yes. The website represents only a portion of Webson's sourcing capabilities. Submit the complete AN, MS, NAS or NASM part number, required quantity and any applicable revision or documentation requirements for review.

What should I include in a Mil-Spec fastener RFQ?

Provide the complete part number, quantity, required delivery date, specification revision when applicable, material or finish requirements and required certifications or traceability documents. Drawings or supporting specifications can be attached to the Webson RFQ form.

Does Webson supply special finishes?

Webson sources Mil-Spec and custom hardware with finishes including cadmium, zinc-nickel and Alodine when required by the applicable specification or drawing. Finish availability depends on the individual part and program requirements.

Which industries does Webson support with Mil-Spec hardware?

Webson supplies hardware for military, aerospace, electronics, medical and other manufacturing applications where specification control, dependable sourcing and traceability are important.
