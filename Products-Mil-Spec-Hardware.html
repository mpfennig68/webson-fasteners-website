// PURCHASING QUESTIONS

Before you request a quote.

Can Webson quote a part that is not listed here?

Yes. Our reference tables cover selected hardware, not our full sourcing range. Send the complete part number, quantity, required delivery date and any source or documentation requirements for review.

Are the stocked tables a live inventory feed?

No. Entries marked Stocked identify selected items Webson stocks. Contact us for current availability, quantity, pricing and lead time before placing an order.

What documentation should I request?

List the documents required by your purchase order: distributor or manufacturer certificates, material or test reports, and lot or heat traceability as applicable. Webson will review availability and the requested scope during quotation.

How do I send a drawing or a multi-line RFQ?

Paste the part list, quantities and requirements into our RFQ form. Email drawings, spreadsheets or supporting specifications to info@websonfasteners.com and include your company name and part number so we can match the files to your inquiry.
