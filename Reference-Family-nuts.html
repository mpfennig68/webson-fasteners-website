// IDENTIFICATION

Check the locking feature and envelope.

Plain, slotted, prevailing-torque and nonmetallic-insert nuts differ in more than appearance. Height, wrench size and bearing features can matter in an existing assembly.

MS21042 is a reduced-height locking family. A regular-height or insert-style locknut should be reviewed against the drawing rather than selected by matching thread size alone.

// PREPARE AN RFQ

What to confirm on the drawing

Thread and class; locking style; height and wrench clearance; material; finish; specified temperature/performance.

  • Complete designation, including all material, finish and locking suffixes.
  • Quantity, required delivery date and controlling drawing or specification issue.
  • Required certificates, source restrictions and lot traceability.
