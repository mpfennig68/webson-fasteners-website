Components for fixture assembly and mounting.

Lighting manufacturers rely on Webson for hardware engineered to withstand heat, vibration, and years of continuous service.

Lighting fixtures run hot, sit outdoors, and vibrate on poles and structures for years without service. Webson supplies fastening and assembly components sized and finished for fixture housings, mounting brackets, and driver enclosures that have to survive all three.

Applications we support

  • Architectural and commercial fixtures
  • Outdoor and street lighting
  • LED driver enclosures
  • Stadium and high-bay lighting
  • Decorative fixture assembly

Fasteners & hardware for lighting manufacturing

Our lighting hardware line is selected for heat, vibration, and outdoor exposure:

  • Thermally stable fasteners: for heat-generating fixture housings
  • Vibration-resistant screws & lock washers: for pole and structure mounting
  • Gaskets & seals: weatherproofing for outdoor fixtures
  • Standoffs & brackets: driver and PCB mounting
  • Corrosion-resistant plating: for long outdoor service life
