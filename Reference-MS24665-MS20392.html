// PIN IDENTIFICATION

Two different retention components

A split cotter pin and a headed, drilled-shank pin are different components. A headed pin may have a hole for a separate retainer; that feature does not make it a cotter pin.

// ASSEMBLY REVIEW

Keep the pin and retainer requirements separate

A drawing may call out both a pin and a retaining component. Confirm the full callouts, their materials and their dimensions together. A hole that accepts a retainer is not itself approval to change the retainer type.
