// IDENTIFICATION

Keep wire requirements separate from fastener requirements.

Safety or lock wire is a separate item used with an assembly-specific installation method. The specified alloy, diameter and condition should accompany the wire designation.

A drilled fastener provides a feature for an intended retention method. Its presence does not establish the wire material, routing or installation requirement.

// PREPARE AN RFQ

What to confirm on the drawing

Full wire designation; diameter; alloy; condition; packaging; applicable installation/process instruction.

  • Complete designation, including all material, finish and locking suffixes.
  • Quantity, required delivery date and controlling drawing or specification issue.
  • Required certificates, source restrictions and lot traceability.
