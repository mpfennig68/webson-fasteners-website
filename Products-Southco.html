// WORK WITH WEBSON

Southco purchasing questions

Can I request a quote for a part that is not listed?

Yes. Send the complete Southco part number, quantity and required delivery date. Webson will review the requirement and available sourcing options.

Do these pages show live inventory?

Request a quote for current pricing, availability and lead time. The product directory provides identification details for purchasing.

Can similarly named Southco parts replace each other?

Check the complete designation and installation requirements first. Panel thickness, grip, finish, mounting style and access restriction can differ within the same product family.

What should I include with a production RFQ?

Provide a line for each part number and quantity, plus delivery dates, annual usage and required certificates. Include any applicable drawing notes or source restrictions.
