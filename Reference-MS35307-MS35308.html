// SOURCING DETAILS

Material and finish remain part of the requirement

A hex head shape and common nominal size do not establish the same material, performance or protective treatment. Preserve the exact series and full designation rather than reducing a specification-controlled part to a commercial description.

// DRAWING REVIEW

Before reviewing an alternate

Confirm pitch, thread class, length, head dimensions, material and required documentation. Where a drawing calls for the legacy designation, state that requirement exactly and identify any required customer approval before ordering a changed part.

// BUYER CHECKLIST

Prepare the requirement for quotation

  • Complete designation, including all material, finish and locking suffixes.
  • Quantity, required delivery date and controlling drawing or specification issue.
  • Required certificates, source restrictions and lot traceability.
