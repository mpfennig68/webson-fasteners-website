Self-clinching fasteners for sheet-metal assemblies.

A self-clinching fastener becomes part of the sheet through the specified installation process. The sheet and prepared hole are part of the selection, not just the thread. Send Webson the complete manufacturer number or the fastener and panel drawings for quotation.

Check the fastener and panel drawings together

Two fasteners with the same thread can need different panel thicknesses, holes or sheet conditions. Preserve the series and all size codes. The selected manufacturer’s installation data controls hole preparation, edge distance, tooling and installation requirements.

Keep installation requirements with the part number

Include fastener material and finish along with the panel details. If the panel manufacturing process or coating sequence is controlled, send that information too. Do not substitute a different clinching type simply because the projecting or internal thread matches.

Panel fastening for electronics and equipment

Self-clinching hardware is used on chassis, enclosures, brackets and other sheet-metal parts. For a replacement, identify the original product and panel condition. For new production, provide the installation drawing and acceptance requirements with the bill of material.

Before you order

Is any press-in fastener a self-clinching fastener?

No. Different attachment designs can use different hole, sheet and installation requirements. Identify the actual product family.

Can I select one from thread size alone?

No. Include the sheet requirements, hole preparation and complete series or shank designation.

Can a fastener intended for one sheet material be used in another?

Check the exact product’s permitted material and hardness conditions. A change requires review of the full installation requirement.

Send the panel details with the fastener requirement

Include the full manufacturer number or current drawings, quantity and delivery date. Add sheet material, thickness, hardness where controlled, hole details, finish and required documentation.
