// FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Commercial fastener questions.

Does Webson supply both inch and metric fasteners?

Yes. Webson supplies and sources commercial fasteners in standard inch and metric thread systems. Include the required diameter, thread, length, material, finish and quantity with the RFQ.

Which fastener materials does Webson supply?

Webson’s commercial fastener range includes steel, stainless steel, brass and nylon. Other material requirements can be reviewed according to the applicable part number, drawing and quantity.

Can Webson source fasteners that are not in stock?

Yes. Webson stocks many common commercial fasteners and can source non-stock sizes, materials, finishes and specialty configurations through its manufacturing network.

Can I submit a complete purchase list or bill of material?

Yes. Customers can submit individual part numbers, recurring purchase lists or complete bills of material. Webson can review requirements across commercial, electronic, custom and Mil-Spec product categories.

Can Webson supply special finishes?

Webson can review requirements for different commercial and application-specific finishes. Include the required finish, specification and any supporting documentation with the RFQ.

Can commercial fasteners be included in a vendor-managed inventory program?

Yes. Recurring fastener requirements can be evaluated for vendor-managed inventory, bin stocking, custom kitting and scheduled-release programs.

What information should I include with a commercial fastener RFQ?

Provide the fastener type, diameter, thread, length, material, grade or property class, finish, quantity and required delivery date whenever available. Part numbers, drawings, purchase lists and supporting specifications can be attached to Webson’s RFQ form.
