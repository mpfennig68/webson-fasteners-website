// SIZE COMPARISON

One fastener size can have different washer proportions

MS15795-805 and MS15795-806 both reference a #6 fastener and a nominal 0.156-inch inside diameter. Their nominal outside diameters are 0.312 and 0.375 inch, with thicknesses of 0.035 and 0.049 inch respectively.

The nominal values support identification. Drawing limits govern inspection and acceptance.

// FINISH IDENTIFICATION

Retain the finish suffix

The selected unsuffixed stainless entries are passivated. A terminal B identifies the black-oxide finish in the selected stocked parts, including MS15795-803B, MS15795-806B and MS15795-808B.

Keep that suffix in the quote request so the required finish follows the part number.
