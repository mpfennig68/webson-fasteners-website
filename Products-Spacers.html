How to specify spacers

Use the part drawing to define the full geometry. A screw’s nominal thread size is not the same as a spacer’s bore diameter; specify the required clearance rather than assuming those dimensions match.

PCB, panel and chassis applications

Spacers support PCB mounting, separate chassis panels and establish offsets in instruments and equipment enclosures. Check the required bearing area and assembly loads, particularly where a spacer contacts a circuit board. Electrical isolation or grounding depends on the complete assembly, including fasteners and finishes.

For repeat production requirements, vendor-managed inventory and custom kitting can be reviewed against your bill of material and release schedule.

Questions before ordering

What is the difference between a spacer and a standoff?

A plain sleeve spacer has an unthreaded bore and is commonly used around a through-fastener. A threaded standoff provides one or more threaded attachment ends. Catalog terminology can vary, so specify the bore or thread and end configuration.

Can I specify a spacer by screw size alone?

No. Provide the bore diameter or manufacturer part number along with length, outside diameter, material and finish. The mating screw size alone does not define the required clearance or body dimensions.

Can Webson quote nylon and metal spacers?

Webson can review nylon, aluminum, brass, stainless steel and other specified spacer requirements. Material, finish, quantity and part dimensions determine sourcing options and availability.

Can you review custom spacer lengths or bores?

Yes. Send the current drawing with dimensions, tolerances, material, finish, quantity and requested delivery date. Webson coordinates made-to-print sourcing through manufacturing partners.
