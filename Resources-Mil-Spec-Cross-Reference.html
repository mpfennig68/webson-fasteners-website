// HOW TO READ THE TABLE

Three different meanings of “cross-reference”

Document succession

The official record identifies a successor standard. The table above records this relationship.

Part-number mapping

A notice or standard explicitly relates complete part numbers. Check its tables, exceptions and revision before ordering.

Potential alternate

A supplier suggests a candidate for review. Engineering and quality requirements determine whether it can be accepted.

// PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

Why the MS prefix does not tell the whole story

MS35206 is identified as superseded by NASM35206, while the DLA record for MS51957 remains active. Check the individual document instead of assuming every MS series has been renamed.

Likewise, a drawing may still call out an older designation. Provide that exact designation and the required revision so Webson can review the purchasing requirement.

// BUYER CHECKLIST

Before requesting a replacement

  1. Record the complete number on the drawing or purchase order.
  2. Check the controlling specification, revision and any cancellation notice.
  3. Compare the full designation, material, finish, dimensions and documentation requirements.
  4. Obtain required customer approval for an alternate or changed designation.
  5. State the accepted requirement clearly on the order.

Document cancellation describes the standard’s status; it does not by itself determine whether existing parts are acceptable for an order.
