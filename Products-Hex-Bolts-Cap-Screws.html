Check grip and engagement as well as overall fit

Before you order

Are a hex bolt and a hex cap screw always interchangeable?

Do not assume so. Head, bearing, tolerance and other requirements can differ between standards. Preserve the specified designation.

Does a longer bolt always have more thread?

Thread length depends on the standard and product. Check the actual threaded extent and unthreaded grip.

Can Webson quote MS hex cap screws?

Yes. Send the complete designation and required issue, or use the existing Mil-Spec references to identify a published selection.

Send size, grade and finish together

Include the complete designation or drawing revision, quantity, delivery date, thread, length, grade and finish. Add thread-length controls, source restrictions and required documents.
