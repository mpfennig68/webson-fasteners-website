// PART IDENTIFICATION

Preserve the complete A or AB designation

NAS1399 covers 100-degree flush-head locked-spindle blind rivets. The A and AB designations identify distinct configurations with different installation-tooling requirements. Keep these letters in the quote request.

// PART-NUMBER EXAMPLES

Read material, diameter and grip together

The selected B entry is aluminum; the selected C entries are A286 corrosion-resistant steel. NAS1399B4AB2 and NAS1399C5AB2 differ in both material and diameter.

Use each listed grip range rather than decoding the last number as a universal fraction. The minimum grip for the selected AB2 entries varies with diameter.

// PURCHASING CHECK

Confirm tooling before ordering

Manufacturer guidance distinguishes the A configuration from the AB configuration and its tooling. Confirm the complete rivet designation, approved installation process and compatible pulling head with the applicable tooling documentation.

A shared nominal diameter does not approve a change in material, grip range or locking construction.
