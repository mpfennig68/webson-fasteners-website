Dimensions are minimum–maximum limits in inches. Width across flats describes the nut, not the tolerance of a wrench or socket. Thickness is the nut’s axial height.

// ORDERING EXAMPLES

Read the complete designation

MS25082-3 and MS25082-12 both use 10-32 UNF-2B threads, but their widths across flats are 0.362–0.375 and 0.241–0.250 inch respectively. MS25082-C3 preserves the -3 geometry while selecting passivated 303 stainless. A common thread alone does not identify the same hex envelope.

// MATERIAL AND FINISH

Preserve the material and finish

The selected bare numeric dash numbers identify cadmium-plated steel. C followed by the size dash number identifies passivated Type 303 stainless steel in the reviewed table. Preserve the C: MS25082-1 and MS25082-C1 are different material/finish configurations. Include the required coating specification and issue with the quote.

// FAMILY COMPARISON

Compare thread, envelope and part status

Thickness matters even when the thread and hex match. MS25082-7 and -20 both have 3/8-32 UNEF-2B threads and the same across-flats limits, but use 0.078 and 0.093-inch nominal thicknesses, each with ±0.005-inch tolerance. The 0.4688-32 entries retain the drawing’s decimal nominal thread reference; do not round that callout to another nominal size.
