Commercial Hardware

Bolts, screws, studs, nuts, and washers in steel, stainless, brass, and nylon, standard inch and metric, stocked for immediate release. Whatever the job calls for, we carry it across every common material and finish, and anything we don’t stock, we source fast.

Electronic Hardware

Handles, knobs, spacers, and standoffs for chassis, panels, and PCBs, machined and molded, conductive or insulating. From a single connector to a full panel’s worth of hardware, we keep the complete range on the shelf so you can source it all in one place.

HANDLES · KNOBS · SPACERS · STANDOFFS · BUSHINGS · LATCHES

Electronic Assembly Components

Rivets, grommets, and clamps that hold assemblies together and protect wiring through vibration and thermal cycling. Whatever your assembly needs, we supply the full complement of components to match, so there’s never a reason to split the order across vendors.

RIVETS · GROMMETS · CLAMPS · GASKETS · O-RINGS · CABLE TIES

Mil-Spec Hardware

AN, MS, NAS, and NASM hardware to full military specification, supplied with aerospace-grade traceability to AS9120B. Whatever the drawing specifies, we source it to spec with complete documentation. One supplier covers your entire mil-spec bill of materials. See where these parts are used on our Military & Aerospace industry page.

Custom Fasteners & Finishes
