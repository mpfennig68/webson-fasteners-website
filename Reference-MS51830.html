// FAMILY SELECTION

Choose the insert family before the thread

MS51830 identifies miniature and lightweight keylocking inserts. The outside thread and insert envelope must suit the parent component as well as the screw entering the insert. MS51831 and MS51832 cover different duty families, so an internal-thread match alone does not establish an equivalent outside fit.

MS51830Miniature and lightweight

// PART-NUMBER EXAMPLES

Material and internal locking notation

MS51830F uses the separator dash for 303/303Se corrosion-resistant steel, A in its place for the specified alloy-steel options and CA for A286. An L after the dash number specifies the internal thread-lock feature.

MS51830F designation examples
ItemReference
MS51830-106Dash material notation; dash number 106; no L suffix
MS51830-106LSame base selection with an internal locking feature
MS51830CA106LCA material notation, dash 106 and internal locking

Read these as examples from revision F, not as a universal suffix rule for other insert families.
