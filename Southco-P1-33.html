// FIT & SELECTION

What to check before ordering

Check the panel cutout, thickness and rear clearance for the cup. The screw-mounted P1 arrangement differs from the snap-in P2 style even when both are described as flush pulls.

Include the full manufacturer part number on your RFQ. If your drawing adds a revision, approved-source restriction or documentation requirement, send those details with the inquiry.
