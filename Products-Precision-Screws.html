Keep shoulder length, screw length and thread length separate

For many projecting-head screws, length is measured from beneath the head. Countersunk screws commonly use an overall length convention. Shoulder screws have a separate shoulder-length callout. Follow the selected standard or manufacturer drawing rather than transferring one convention to another.

Put the tolerance on the drawing

If a shoulder locates a moving part, send the mating bore or fit requirement with the screw drawing. If head height is critical under a cover, show the available envelope. These details help identify a suitable catalog part or establish what a custom supplier must make.

Standard and custom screws in one inquiry

Include standard fasteners and drawing-specific items in the same bill of material. For a standard item, preserve the full designation and required issue. For a custom screw, include revision, quantity and the dimensions or processes that need inspection.

Before you order

Does precision mean a particular tolerance?

No. The term needs a drawing or specification behind it. State the dimensions and tolerances required for the actual part.

Can a machine screw replace a shoulder screw with the same thread?

The shoulder supplies geometry that a fully threaded screw does not. Compare the complete design and obtain approval for any proposed substitution.

Can Webson quote an MS screw?

Yes. Use the Mil-Spec references to identify a published selection, or send the complete designation and required specification issue with your inquiry.

Send the designation or the controlled drawing

Include the full part number or drawing revision, quantity, requested delivery date, material, finish and documentation. Call out any critical dimensions or inspection reports required for acceptance.
