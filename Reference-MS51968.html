// ORDERING EXAMPLES

Read the complete part number

MS51968-2 is a 1/4-28 Grade B reference; MS51968-3 is Grade C. MS51968-14 is a 1/2-20 Grade B reference; MS51968-15 is Grade C. The diameter and grade can match a coarse-thread nut while the pitch remains different.

// MATERIAL AND FINISH

Retain the material and finish requirement

The selected rows are cadmium-plated carbon steel in Grade B or Grade C. The reviewed specification requires finished nuts to meet the proof-load requirements of its procurement specification. The grade letters belong to the material classification; the listed ordering number uses the MS series and dash number. Do not infer proof load from dimensions alone.
