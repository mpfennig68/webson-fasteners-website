// ASSEMBLY COMPONENTS

The supporting components behind a reliable assembly.

Electronic and mechanical assemblies often require dozens of supporting components beyond their primary fasteners. Webson supplies and sources the hardware used to join parts, organize wiring, protect openings, retain components and seal equipment.

Customers can submit individual part numbers, drawings, samples or a complete bill of material for review.

Rivets and Rivet Nuts

Permanent and semi-permanent fastening components used to join panels, sheet materials and assemblies or create threaded attachment points.

Grommets

Components used to protect wires, cables, tubing and other materials where they pass through panels or openings.

Clamps

Hardware used to secure, support and route wires, cables, tubing and other components within an assembly.

Gaskets

Components used between mating surfaces to provide sealing, cushioning, spacing or environmental protection.

O-Rings

Circular sealing components supplied according to the required dimensions, material and application requirements.

Cable Ties

Components used to bundle, organize and retain wiring, cables and other items within equipment and assemblies.

Clips and Retaining Components

Hardware used to position, retain, guide or secure parts without a conventional threaded fastener.

Do not see the component you need? Send Webson the manufacturer part number, dimensions, drawing or application requirements for review.

// COMPONENT FUNCTIONS

Joining, protection and organization within the assembly.

The correct assembly component depends on the function it must perform and the environment in which it will be used. Webson can source components for several common assembly functions.

Joining

Rivets, rivet nuts and related components used to fasten panels, brackets, housings and other assembly elements.

Routing and Organization

Clamps, cable ties and supporting hardware used to manage wires, cables, tubing and harnesses.

Edge and Pass-Through Protection

Grommets and bushings used to protect materials passing through sheet metal, panels and enclosure openings.

Sealing and Cushioning

Gaskets and O-rings used between components where sealing, cushioning or separation is required.

Retention and Positioning

Clips, clamps and retaining components used to hold parts in their intended locations.

The customer’s part number, drawing and application requirements determine the correct component. Webson does not replace the customer’s engineering or design authority.

// SELECTION CONSIDERATIONS

Specified for the component, opening and environment.

Assembly components are selected according to their dimensions, material, mounting method, surrounding components and operating environment. Providing complete information helps Webson identify and quote the correct product.

✓

Dimensions

Provide component dimensions, panel thickness, hole size, groove size or other relevant measurements whenever available.

✓

Material

Identify the required metal, plastic, rubber or other material when it is controlled by the drawing or application.

✓

Mounting Method

Specify whether the component is pushed in, snapped in, threaded, riveted, bolted, tied or otherwise retained.

✓

Environmental Requirements

Include any known requirements related to temperature, vibration, moisture, chemicals, corrosion or outdoor exposure.

✓

Part Number or Drawing

Manufacturer part numbers, customer part numbers and drawings provide the best basis for accurate sourcing.

✓

Quantity and Packaging

Include the required order quantity and any special packaging, labeling or kitting requirements.

When replacing an existing component, provide the complete manufacturer part number or an accurate drawing whenever possible.

// FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Electronic assembly component questions.

Which electronic assembly components does Webson supply?

Webson supplies and sources rivets, rivet nuts, grommets, clamps, gaskets, O-rings, cable ties, clips and related supporting hardware for electronic, mechanical and equipment assemblies.

Can Webson source an existing manufacturer part number?

Yes. Submit the complete manufacturer part number, customer part number, required quantity and any applicable documentation requirements for review.

What information is needed to quote a grommet or clamp?

Provide the part number or drawing whenever available. Relevant information may include the component dimensions, panel thickness, hole size, cable or tubing diameter, material, mounting method and required quantity.

Can Webson supply custom assembly components?

Webson can review drawings for custom dimensions, materials and configurations. Manufacturing options and minimum quantities depend on the component and production requirements.

Can assembly components be included in a kit?

Yes. Fasteners, rivets, grommets, clamps and related components can be evaluated for custom kitting and packaging based on the customer’s assembly or bill of material.

Can these products be included in a vendor-managed inventory program?

Yes. Recurring assembly components can be evaluated for vendor-managed inventory, bin stocking and scheduled-release programs.

What information should I include with an assembly component RFQ?

Provide the manufacturer part number or drawing, required quantity, dimensions, material, application requirements, documentation requirements and requested delivery date whenever available.
