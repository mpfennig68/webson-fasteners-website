// DIMENSION SELECTION

Length and grip are different dimensions

AN4-25A: 2.656-inch under-head length and 2.188-inch unthreaded grip.

Under-head length includes the threaded end. Grip identifies the unthreaded shank portion. The tabulated decimal references are rounded to three places; full tolerances and transitions remain controlled by the drawing.

// PART-NUMBER DETAILS

Preserve the drilling identifiers

AN4-27 has a drilled shank. AN4-23A, -24A, -25A and -26A have undrilled shanks. Preserve the A ending where specified.

Drilling features support the retention arrangement specified for the assembly. Confirm that arrangement against the controlling installation requirements.
