// IDENTIFICATION

Match both internal and external requirements.

The screw that enters an insert and the hole that receives it are two separate interfaces. Wall thickness, locking method and installation tooling must be considered with both threads.

MS51830, MS51831 and MS51832 identify lightweight, heavy-duty and extra-heavy-duty keylocking families. The internal thread size alone does not define the same outside envelope.

// PREPARE AN RFQ

What to confirm on the drawing

Internal/external thread; wall/duty series; length; key arrangement; internal locking; material; installation tooling.

  • Complete designation, including all material, finish and locking suffixes.
  • Quantity, required delivery date and controlling drawing or specification issue.
  • Required certificates, source restrictions and lot traceability.
