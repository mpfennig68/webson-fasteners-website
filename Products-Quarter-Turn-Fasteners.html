Use the manufacturer’s grip definition

Grip or total material thickness can include the panel, frame and specified accessory items under the selected system’s definition. It is not safe to substitute a single panel-thickness measurement. Use the series instructions and complete installed stack.

Preserve the system and finish requirements

Include the full manufacturer series and finish for each component, with any source restrictions. If replacing one part, identify the mating hardware that stays in the assembly. A change in stud or receptacle can change the required selection and installation.

Service covers and repeated access

Quarter-turn systems can provide the specified access arrangement for equipment panels and covers. Confirm whether components remain retained when the panel is opened and what must clear during removal. List all required parts on the inquiry.

Before you order

Is the stud length selected from panel thickness alone?

Use the selected system’s full grip or stack definition and mating receptacle data.

Are receptacles interchangeable between series?

Do not assume compatibility. Preserve the matched system or obtain approval for a complete change.

Is a quarter-turn fastener the same as any quick-access screw?

No. Different systems use different engagement and operating arrangements. Identify the actual series and mating hardware.

Quote the full panel fastening system

Send the stud, receptacle and accessory part numbers, quantities and delivery date. If numbers are unknown, include panel/frame drawings, the defined stack, operating style, material and documentation requirements.
