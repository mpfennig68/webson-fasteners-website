Keep ring dimensions separate from groove dimensions

Inside diameter and cross-section describe the uninstalled O-ring. Groove depth, width and mating clearances describe the gland. Do not use one set of dimensions in place of the other when identifying a replacement.

“Rubber” is not a purchasing specification

Include the approved compound and any required certification, cure-date or packaging controls. If the original designation is missing, send the application conditions and available drawings for review. Avoid choosing a replacement solely because its color and dimensions look right.

Replacement seals and production requirements

For established equipment, the approved part list is the best starting point. For a new assembly, provide the seal requirement and gland details. This page helps identify information for quotation; it does not replace the seal design or compatibility review.

Before you order

Does a dash size specify the O-ring material?

The size designation and compound are separate requirements unless a complete specification combines them. Preserve the full purchasing designation.

Can I identify the compound from its color?

Do not rely on color alone. Use the manufacturer’s designation, approved specification or documented material requirement.

Should I measure a stretched or worn ring?

It may help identify a candidate size, but stretching, wear and service exposure can change dimensions. Confirm against the original requirement before ordering.

Quote the size and compound as one requirement

Send the full part number or size, compound, hardness, quantity and delivery date. Include the applicable specification, documentation, packaging and shelf-life controls required by the order.
