## Editorial policy

## New buyer guides: research and editorial scope

Reviewed October 18, 2026. Public abstracts and government reference material support high-level descriptions only. No licensed standard text, process recipes, acceptance criteria, universal substitution tables or torque values are reproduced. RFQ fields and FAQ recommendations are Webson editorial purchasing guidance, not a claim of a regulatory requirement.

- `Resources-Mil-Spec-RFQ-Checklist.html`: based on the existing Quote.html fields and established sourcing/quality requirements. Existing form accepts a combined 10 MB attachment limit. No form handler or submission behavior changed.
- `Resources-Mil-Spec-Materials-Finishes.html`: material / condition / surface-treatment distinction; treatment terminology; MS51830 example remains tied to revision G requirements 2, 3 and 7.
- `Resources-Mil-Spec-Purchasing-FAQ.html`: existing verified document relationships and hardware distinctions, plus editorial RFQ/documentation guidance. No general availability or alternate-approval promises.

### Additional primary references

- SAE AMS2700G, Passivation of Corrosion Resistant Steels (public scope): https://saemobilus.sae.org/standards/ams2700g-passivation-corrosion-resistant-steels . Supports removal of free iron / less noble surface contaminants, not a plating process or process instructions.
- ASTM A967/A967M-25 (public abstract): https://store.astm.org/a0967_a0967m-25.html . Supports the category of chemical passivation treatments; public page does not establish a treatment choice for an individual part.
- SAE AMSQQP416G (public title/scope): https://saemobilus.sae.org/standards/amsqqp416g-plating-cadmium-electrodeposited . Supports cadmium electrodeposition terminology only; retain order-specific type/class.
- SAE AS5272G (public title/scope): https://saemobilus.sae.org/standards/as5272g-lubricant-solid-film-heat-cured-corrosion-inhibiting-procurement-specification . Supports solid-film lubricant terminology, not interchangeability of lubricant types.
- NASA RP-1228, Fastener Design Manual (1990): https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19900009424 . Historical engineering background for separating material, coating and lubricant; not a current procurement standard.
- DLA ASSIST Quick Search Help: https://quicksearch.dla.mil/qsHelp.aspx . Document-status terminology and revision context; no automatic complete-part conversion.
