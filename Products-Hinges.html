Review the closed, open and removed positions

Compare the pivot and mounting surfaces to the enclosure drawing. Check clearance throughout the movement, not just at full opening. A removable hinge also needs room for the specified separation path.

Specify the complete hinge arrangement

For a replacement, include the full manufacturer number and orientation. For a new design, provide panel details, load and positioning requirements, mounting arrangement and service conditions. A hinge’s appearance does not establish its torque or load rating.

Access panels and serviceable enclosures

Hinges support access doors, instrument panels and equipment covers. Removable and positioning designs solve different access needs. Include the latch and any clearance constraints when the closure hardware is being reviewed as one assembly.

Before you order

Can I choose a hinge by hole spacing alone?

Also compare pivot location, installed offset, movement, handedness and load requirements. Hole spacing does not define the full installation.

Does a positioning hinge have the same torque in both directions?

That depends on the exact product. Preserve the torque and direction designation from the manufacturer data.

Can Webson quote Southco hinges?

Yes. Browse the existing Southco pages or send the full number, quantity and delivery requirement. Include matching or handed parts if the assembly needs them.
