// THICKNESS SELECTION

Keep the light-series ending

L identifies the light series. NAS620C4L is 0.016 inch thick; NAS620C4 is 0.032 inch thick. NAS620C10 is a #10 selection, while NAS620C416 references a 1/4-inch screw size.

// MATERIAL IDENTIFICATION

Retain the material notation

The selected C entries identify passivated corrosion-resistant steel, A identifies aluminum alloy, and a hyphen identifies cadmium-plated steel. Keep the complete designation when ordering.
