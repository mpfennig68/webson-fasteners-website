// PART IDENTIFICATION

Regular-height nonmetallic-insert construction

MS21044 identifies regular-height hexagon self-locking nuts with a nonmetallic insert. The locking construction and height envelope belong with the thread and material in the ordering requirement.

// PART-NUMBER EXAMPLES

Read the material and size codes separately

MS21044C06 references a passivated CRES selection with a 6-32 thread. MS21044N04 references a cadmium-plated steel selection with a 4-40 thread.

The N3 and N4 entries identify 10-32 and 1/4-28 threads respectively. Preserve the significant leading zeroes in the smaller size codes.

// FAMILY COMPARISON

Compare locking construction and height

MS21042 is a reduced-height all-metal locking family. MS21083 is a low-height nonmetallic-insert family. Their envelopes and locking mechanisms have separate requirements.
