// FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Medical device hardware questions.

What hardware does Webson supply for medical devices?

Webson supplies and sources stainless and passivated fasteners, tamper-resistant and security screws, captive panel hardware and related components for device housings, enclosures and supporting assemblies.

Does Webson supply traceability documentation?

Traceability and order documentation can be supplied according to the applicable component and customer requirements. State all documentation requirements with the RFQ and purchase order.

Is Webson certified?

Webson is certified to AS9120B and ISO 9001 for the distribution of fastening products and installation tooling. View the certifications.

Can Webson source custom or drawing-based components?

Webson can review customer drawings and coordinate production through qualified manufacturing sources. Manufacturing options and minimum quantities depend on the component and production requirements.

What information should I include with an RFQ?

Provide the manufacturer part number or drawing, required quantity, dimensions, material, finish, documentation requirements and requested delivery date whenever available. Drawings and supporting specifications can be attached to Webson’s RFQ form.
