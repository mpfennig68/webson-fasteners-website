// ORDERING EXAMPLES

Read the complete designation

MS35690-424 is a 1/4-28 Grade B carbon-steel nut, uncoated; MS35690-427 is Grade C. MS35690-413 is a 1/4-28 nickel-plated brass nut. MS35690-613 is the nickel-plated brass 3/8-16 selection, while -633 is 3/8-24. Keep the material, finish and thread together with the dash number.

// MATERIAL AND FINISH

Preserve the material and finish

The selected carbon-steel nuts are uncoated Grade B or Grade C. The selected brass nuts are nickel plated. Grade B and Grade C are nut material classifications under the procurement requirements; do not relabel them as Grade 5 or Grade 8 bolts or infer proof load from the hex dimensions.
