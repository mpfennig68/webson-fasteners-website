// IDENTIFICATION

Measure the shoulder, not just the thread.

The smooth shoulder provides a locating or bearing surface. Its diameter and length are separate from the threaded end, so a thread-size match alone does not define the same component.

MS51575 is a slotted family; MS51576 uses a hex socket. Matching dash numbers do not always select matching shoulder lengths across these two series.

// PREPARE AN RFQ

What to confirm on the drawing

Shoulder diameter and length; drive; male thread; head clearance; bearing surface and tolerances.

  • Complete designation, including all material, finish and locking suffixes.
  • Quantity, required delivery date and controlling drawing or specification issue.
  • Required certificates, source restrictions and lot traceability.
