// SERIES OVERVIEW

What is an MS51957 screw?

MS51957 identifies pan head, cross-recessed machine screws in corrosion-resistant steel with UNC-2A threads. It is a specification-controlled family: the complete designation and the applicable drawing define the item required for an assembly.

The series is commonly described as stainless pan head machine screws. For purchasing, retain the exact MS number and all suffixes rather than substituting a generic commercial description.

// PART NUMBERS

How to read the ordering requirement

Start with the complete designation on the drawing. The series number alone does not identify a single screw size. Use the part-number tables in the required issue of MS51957 to establish the selected diameter, length and any applicable suffix requirements.

Dash-number systems differ between series. Do not infer a length or material from a similar-looking MS or NAS number, and do not add or remove a finish suffix without checking the specification.

// RELATED SERIES

MS51957 and MS35206

Both series are associated with pan head, cross-recessed machine screws, but their stated material families differ. MS51957 covers corrosion-resistant steel; the MS35206 series description identifies cadmium-plated carbon steel with UNC-2A threads.

The MS35206 document review also identifies NASM35206 as its successor. Shared head and thread descriptions do not make these series interchangeable.
