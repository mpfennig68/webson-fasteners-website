Custom fasteners for electronics and other assemblies

A spacer that sets a PCB height calls for different drawing details than a shoulder bolt or a replacement shaft. Our custom fasteners for electronics page focuses on board spacing, chassis mounting and enclosure hardware.

We also review made-to-print requirements for aerospace and defense, medical equipment, and enclosures and cabinets. Applicable specifications and documentation requirements should accompany the drawing.

Questions before you request a quote

What types of custom fasteners can Webson source?

Webson reviews custom screws, bolts, studs, pins, spacers, standoffs, bushings, sleeves, nuts, inserts and other made-to-print components. Manufacturing options depend on the drawing, material and quantity.

Can standard and custom hardware be quoted together?

Yes. Send a bill of material identifying standard part numbers and the drawings for custom components. Separate quantities and requirements for each line item.

Can you quote a part from a sample or an old part number?

Send the information you have for review. A sample or old part number may help identify the requirement, but production needs agreed dimensions, material, finish and acceptance requirements.

Are prototype quantities available?

Submit the required quantity for review. Minimum quantities, tooling costs and lead times depend on the part and manufacturing source. There is no single minimum for all custom parts.

Send the drawing and the requirements together

Start with the part number, drawing revision, quantity and requested delivery date. Include material, finish, critical tolerances and required certifications. For repeat orders, add annual usage and release quantities if known.
